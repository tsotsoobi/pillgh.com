Privacy Policy

How we handle personal data, in plain language.

Last updated 29 August 2026

Pi Logistics Ltd. is a freight forwarding and logistics company registered in Ghana. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, where it is processed, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

It covers our website and our WhatsApp flight booking service, where you chat with an assistant that searches for flights and holds a seat, and a Pi Logistics agent then contacts you to take payment and issue the ticket.

We never collect payment card details. Our booking assistant cannot take a payment, cannot start a mobile money prompt, and cannot store a card number. Payment is arranged separately by a human agent, and if you ever type a card number into the chat we do not keep it.

1. Who we are

Pi Logistics Ltd., Accra, Ghana. TIN C0062549871. For anything in this policy, write to info@pillgh.com.

2. What we collect

When you book a flight over WhatsApp

Airlines require certain details before they will hold or issue a ticket. We ask only for what the specific fare requires, which is usually:

  • Your name, exactly as it appears on your passport
  • Your date of birth
  • Your gender, as the airline records it
  • Your passport details: number, issuing country and expiry date, when the fare requires identity documents
  • Your WhatsApp phone number, which is how you reached us and how we reply
  • The text of your conversation with the assistant, so it can follow what you have already told it

We also keep a record of the booking itself: the route, the dates, the price we quoted, the airline reference, and what happened to it. That record contains no passport numbers and no dates of birth.

When you use our website

If you send us an enquiry or a tracking request, we keep what you send us and how to reply to you. We do not use advertising trackers.

3. Why we collect it

  • To search for and hold flights. Passenger details are sent to airlines through Duffel, our flight booking supplier, because no airline will hold a seat without them.
  • To reply to you. Your phone number and conversation are used to carry on the conversation and to tell you when your payment has been recorded and your ticket issued.
  • To keep an accurate record of what we quoted, held and issued, which we need for disputes, refunds and our own accounts.

Our legal basis is the performance of a contract with you, and our legitimate interest in keeping accurate business records. Where you have asked us to do something specific, such as watch a fare for you, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. How long we keep it

  • Passenger details (name, date of birth, gender, passport details) are deleted 30 days after your booking closes, or 30 days after a held seat expires if you do not go ahead. Deletion means the record is removed outright, not hidden.
  • Conversation text is held in temporary storage and expires automatically after 24 hours of inactivity.
  • The booking record (route, dates, price, airline reference, what happened) is kept for our accounts and for handling later disputes. It contains no passport numbers and no dates of birth.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you what personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong, such as a misspelled name before a ticket is issued
  • Delete your data, where we are not required to keep it for an active booking or for our accounts
  • Give you a copy of what you gave us, in a form you can take elsewhere
  • Stop a particular use, such as a fare watch you asked for

Write to info@pillgh.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. How we protect it

  • Everything travels over encrypted connections.
  • Passenger details are stored in one place, separate from the rest of our records, so they can be deleted cleanly when their time is up.
  • Our staff dashboard shows a passport by its last three characters only. The full number is never displayed.
  • Access to the dashboard is password protected and limited to Pi Logistics staff handling your booking.

8. Children

Our services are for adults. We book tickets for children when an adult makes the booking, and in that case the adult provides the child's details and is responsible for them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant and affects a booking of yours, we will tell you on WhatsApp.
